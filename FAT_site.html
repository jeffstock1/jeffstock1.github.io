Why FAT? (The History)

FAT was founded in 2018 during a particularly tense hostile takeover bid. Our founder, a brilliant but volatile derivatives trader, accidentally screamed "MARGIN CALL! LIQUIDATE THE BOARD!" during a crucial presentation. Instead of being fired, the other side panicked and folded.

We realized our condition wasn't a liability—it was a chaotic, high-energy, market-moving asset. Today, we train advisors to harness the power of sudden, contextually inappropriate financial screaming. Our motto: *We don't just anticipate market panic; we generate it.*

Fact: Our most successful fund manager only communicates in sudden, one-word shouts. His portfolio performance is surprisingly solid.

Our Ethics (Yes, We Have Them)

Our core commitment is to *transparency*, which means everything we think—the good, the bad, and the nonsensical tax shelters—will eventually be shouted at you without warning. We provide the fastest, most unfiltered access to an advisor's subconscious mind in the entire industry.

FAT's Core Tenets:

  • Acceptance: If we suddenly scream "INSIDER TRADING," we are legally required to clarify if that was a tic or actual advice. (It's usually a tic.)
  • Noise Discipline: We provide complimentary noise-canceling headphones for clients. You'll need them.
  • Aggressive Returns: We are loud. Our portfolios are, too.
